A personal channel management integration

Manage channels with clarity.

Panza Digital YouTube Kit connects the owner’s desktop tools to YouTube to help manage the owner’s channels and understand their performance.

Personal use · Owner-authorized access

What it does

The integration uses the open-source YouTube Kit command-line and MCP tools to work with channel and video information, manage authorized channel content, and read available channel analytics and revenue reports.

How access works

The owner grants access through Google’s authorization screen. YouTube Kit stores its configuration and authorization tokens locally. The owner can review or revoke access through their Google Account.

Who it is for

This desktop integration is operated by Tiago Oliveira for Panza Digital and is intended for the owner’s own YouTube channels. It is not a public sign-in service.
