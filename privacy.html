Panza Digital YouTube Kit

Privacy policy

This policy explains how the owner’s personal desktop integration uses information received through Google and YouTube.

Operator and purpose

Tiago Oliveira operates this integration for Panza Digital to manage the owner’s YouTube channels and review their performance. For questions about this policy or data handling, contact tiagopanza88@gmail.com.

Information and permissions

With the Google Account owner’s consent, the integration requests permission to manage YouTube account and channel content (youtube.force-ssl) and read monetary YouTube Analytics reports (yt-analytics-monetary.readonly). Depending on what the owner asks it to do, it may access channel and video metadata, content and channel-owned analytics or revenue information, and make changes permitted by the granted access.

Google presents the requested permissions during authorization. The integration uses this information to perform the owner’s requested channel-management and reporting tasks.

Storage, processing and sharing

YouTube Kit stores its configuration and authorization tokens locally on the owner’s computer. Local data and credentials remain there until removed, subject to any copies or retention by systems the owner uses. Google processes requests made through its APIs, and information may be processed by the owner’s connected AI tools when the owner requests their help. The operator does not sell Google user data.

This informational website is intended to be hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may collect site access information as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Retention and controls

The owner can revoke the integration’s Google access at Google Account permissions. To remove locally stored credentials and data, delete the YouTube Kit configuration, token files and any other local copies the owner has created. Revoking access and deleting local files are separate actions.

Google policies

The integration’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Google’s own handling of information is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Changes

This policy may be updated if the integration or its data practices change. The current version will be available on this page.
